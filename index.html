Services

Annual service: our main work

  • Annual oil service + multi-point inspection: factory-schedule service with genuine or OE-supplier parts
  • Fluids and filters: brake fluid, cabin and engine air filters, differential and transfer case, ZF transmission, coolant
  • Spark plugs: OE-supplier plugs at the scheduled interval
  • Air suspension health check: leak-down test, compressor duty, ride height
  • Plug-in hybrids (PHEV): we handle routine service. High-voltage battery, charging and hybrid-system work goes to a Land Rover dealer and is often covered under extended warranty.

Diagnosis and repair: also offered

  • Air suspension repair: air springs, compressors and valve blocks
  • Engine and cooling: timing chains, coolant pipes, water pumps, supercharger couplers, oil leaks
  • Brakes: front pads and rotors with OE-supplier parts
One diagnosis or repair vehicle is booked per day.

Recalls and factory-warranty repairs are done free by authorized dealers; we will check your VIN for open recalls and send you there when that applies.
